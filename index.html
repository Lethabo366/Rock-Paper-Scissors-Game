WELCOME TO ROCK, PAPER, SCISSORS!

An evil AI has challenged you!

Can you defeat it before it wins?

GAME RULES

  • Rock beats Scissors.
  • Paper beats Rock.
  • Scissors beats Paper.
  • First to 3 points wins.
  • Draws do not award points.
  • Invalid choices do not count.
  • Press Cancel to quit safely.

HOW TO PLAY

Enter Rock, Paper, or Scissors when the prompt appears.

RESULTS

Each round appears in an alert and in your browser console.

HOW TO OPEN THE CONSOLE

On Windows: Press F12 or Ctrl + Shift + J.

Select the Console tab if needed.

On Mac: Press Cmd + Option + J in Chrome.
